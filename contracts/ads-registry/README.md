# Noncustodial advertising registry

This contract registers commitments and signed canonical publisher attestations. It holds no token balance and contains no transfer, escrow, or release_funds operation. A queued commitment is not delivered advertising. Its trust model is an authorized issuer plus the configured Ed25519 publisher; neither an attestation nor completion proves human attendance or AR views.

All timestamps and TIME/EXCLUSIVE units are integer milliseconds. PASSES units are complete plays; pass_ms fixes the duration. The chain timestamp is converted from seconds to milliseconds, so a keeper may need to wait until the next ledger before submitting a recently completed interval.

API: __constructor(issuer, publisher_public_key); commit(Commitment); record_delivery(Evidence, signature); complete(order_hash); dispute(order_hash); resolve_dispute(order_hash, remedy_hash); expire(order_hash); get(order_hash). commit and dispute require issuer authorization. Anyone may relay a signed evidence item or call complete/expire. A dispute can reopen a completed or expired record for investigation and prevents subsequent automatic closure. An issuer-authorized resolve_dispute records the hash of the explicit remedy, preserves totals and evidence, and returns the record to Queued/Partial. Completion still requires every unit; resolving a complaint cannot manufacture delivery. There is no routine human approval step.

The signed message is the SHA256 digest of Soroban XDR for the tuple (Symbol("ads_delivery_v1"), network_id, current_contract_address, evidence). The publisher signs that 32-byte digest with Ed25519. Nonces must increment from zero per order. Each (order, placement, segment) is unique; intervals are chronological, bounded by the purchased window, nonoverlapping, and cannot exceed contracted units. A complete PASSES evidence item represents exactly one uninterrupted pass. A partial or expired order retains the delivered totals and deficit.

TIME and EXCLUSIVE attestations measure the canonical publisher, not connected devices. The publisher must stop its own accounting during host pauses, safety withdrawal, lost lease or unconfirmed playlist versions. The contract checks the issuer's committed totals and the signature; it cannot independently observe the venue, scheduler capacity or publisher availability. Emergency withdrawal and remedies remain in the service.

The issuer must commit hashes of payment, revision, terms and immutable audited manifest before delivery. Do not put logos, raw briefs, personal data or secrets on chain. A disposable Stellar Testnet deployment is recorded in ../../docs/ads/testnet-registry/. Its synthetic queue record proves the contract write/read, not a USDC payment or ad publication. The application integration remains unconfigured. Configuration without a contract and restricted signer stays disabled. The Node adapter persists a prepared signed envelope and hash before submitting it; an uncertain submission requires reconciliation and never signs a replacement automatically. expire is a callable keeper action; the contract does not run on a timer.

Run local tests: cargo test --offline --manifest-path contracts/ads-registry/Cargo.toml. Build for deployment only after review and separate authorization: cargo build --release --target wasm32v1-none --manifest-path contracts/ads-registry/Cargo.toml. Soroban SDK is pinned to 25.3.1. These tests and sources do not constitute an audit, deployment, or Testnet payment evidence.
